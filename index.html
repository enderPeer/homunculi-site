02 / Under the surface

Small enough
to stay curious.

Homunculi began with the Library of Babel’s twenty-nine symbols: lowercase letters, a space, a comma, and a period. A tiny alphabet becomes a way to study how language emerges from training.

Explore the project ↗
a.

A different unit of language

The candidate moves to byte-pair encoding (BPE), grouping characters into tokens. The experiment will measure what this changes.

b.

A sense of position

Rotary position embeddings give the candidate a different way to represent where tokens sit in a sequence.

c.

Evidence before conclusions

A short pilot comes first. A longer run with a larger training corpus follows, with a comparison after both runs finish.
