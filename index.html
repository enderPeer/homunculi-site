Small language models trained from scratch on our own machines in Berlin, ranked best first on an easy 40-question test (all questions and answers). Each chat opens in its own window. Small score differences are not a reliable overall ranking.

    What the new v5 adds

    V5 is an experimental maths/science specialist. It now routes supported English questions to its trained formats, shows the model's answer, and checks it against independent rules. Corrections are labelled separately.

    English chat test: 16/40, fifth in this small test. The questions and grading rules were unchanged. All 40 answers used the legacy fact/link route; this test did not exercise v5's new calculation routes. See every answer and reviewed grade or the v5 run data.
