Welcome to Homunculi

Homunculi is a small language model built from scratch. It reads and writes only the 29 symbols of the Library of Babel: twenty-six lowercase letters, the space, the comma and the period. Numbers are written out as words.

The chat below runs our newest and strongest model on a home GPU cluster. It answers questions about cryptocurrencies and foreign exchange, and about cybernetics and AI, by quoting its sources, and it chats about everything else.
